How I work through a problem.

Every engagement follows the same loop, whether it's a web app, an APK, or an ICS network.

01

Recon

Map the attack surface — assets, entry points, protocols in play.

02

Discovery

Identify weaknesses through manual testing and automated scanning.

03

Validation

Prove exploitability — not just "possible," but demonstrably real.

04

Mitigation

Turn findings into concrete, prioritized remediation.

05

Automation

Encode the fix into tooling so the same gap doesn't reopen.

"I look beyond ‘can I exploit it?’ — connecting offensive findings to detection, mitigation, and automated defense."
